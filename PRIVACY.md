# Privacy

Casio Pager runs entirely on your phone.

- **Notification access** is used to read incoming notifications on the device to decide whether to beep the watch.
  The text of messages is never stored, logged or sent anywhere. Sender names are kept only in your rules and in the
  "recently seen" list on the phone, so you can pick them. The same goes for the titles of group chats you allow.
- **Recent decisions**: the last 30 verdicts (app and reason) are held in memory only, with no names or text.
- **Steps**: the watch's step and distance counters are read over Bluetooth and kept as a daily history on the phone
  (up to 400 days). They are never logged or sent anywhere.
- **Installed apps**: the app can see which apps have a launcher icon, only to show their names in the Rules screen.
- **Bluetooth** is used only to talk to the watch you paired.
- **Log**: a rolling log on the phone (about a day) records link events and why an alert was or was not sent. It holds
  no message text and no names. You can share or clear it from Settings > Logs.
- **Network**: the app makes no network requests by default. Only if you open a diagnostics setup link and confirm
  "Send diagnostics to <host>?" does it push that same log to that server. Only the host is ever shown; you can switch it
  off or remove the server in Settings > Diagnostics (unlocked by tapping the version seven times).
- **Backup**: cloud backup of the app's data is turned off.
- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting.
